The Story

About Saurabh

Stand-up comedian, writer, and storyteller Saurabh Zinzuvadiya (@funnysaurabh) is one of the most exciting and relatable comedic voices emerging from Western India.

With his signature high-energy stage presence, sharp self-deprecating wit, and uncanny talent for finding hilarity in the mundane, Saurabh turns everyday Gujarati household dynamics, WhatsApp family group roasts, and modern middle-class absurdities into thunderous auditorium laughter.

Over the past 6 years, Saurabh has performed in over 400+ live stage shows across 25+ cities in India, including headline comedy specials at leading auditoriums in Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Delhi, Pune, and Bengaluru. He is also a regular headline performer at premier corporate galas and college cultural festivals such as IIM Ahmedabad, IIT Bombay, and Nirma University.

His comedic philosophy is simple: clean, high-impact storytelling that connects as deeply with college students in the front row as it does with grandparents sitting in the balcony.
